# --- 3. ROOT CAUSE: describe says WHAT (exit 1); logs say WHY ---
$ kubectl logs crash-demo --tail=5
Application starting...
Something went wrong!

# The container prints an error and calls exit 1. restartPolicy defaults to
# Always, so the kubelet restarts it, it exits again, and the backoff grows
# (10s, 20s, 40s...). CrashLoopBackOff is the WAIT between restarts, not a crash.
# --- 4. FIX: make the process stay alive ---
